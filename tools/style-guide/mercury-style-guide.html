The Brand

Quiet confidence, not private equity gloss

Mercury buys and operates founder-owned businesses forever. The visual language reflects that promise: a deep, institutional navy that signals permanence; a single vivid orange that signals energy and conviction; and a serif italic that adds the human, personal register — the founder's voice inside the institution's.

Permanent

Sharp corners, hairline rules, no drop shadows, no gradients. Nothing trendy, nothing that will look dated in five years. Radius is 0 across the entire system — treat that as a brand rule, not a default.

Personal

The Freight italic inside headlines is the signature move. It marks the emotional payoff of every message — founder-owned businesses, maintaining your legacy — in the founder's own voice.

Disciplined

One accent, used sparingly: orange appears in eyebrow taglines and rare emphasis moments, never as large surfaces or button fills. Whitespace is generous — 7rem section padding on desktop.

Reference DNA

The Vista lineage

Mercury's look and feel is modeled on Vista Equity Partners — and the connection runs deeper than inspiration. Vista's stylesheet uses the identical palette hex-for-hex and the same two typefaces (Causten + Freight Big Pro). That makes Vista the authoritative reference for how these tokens behave at scale. Below: what their site does that ours doesn't yet, and where Mercury should deliberately diverge.

Craft details to adopt
  • Headline metrics: display sans at weight 200, letter-spacing −1.6px, line-height 1; the serif em runs 1.07× larger with +1.7px tracking so both faces sit at the same optical size. (Our current build uses positive tracking on Causten — Vista's negative tracking is noticeably more confident at 60px+.)
  • Type scale ceiling: display 80px / display-lg 98px, paragraph clamp(18px → 26px), meta 12px, stats up to 143px. Taller than our current 84px H1 — gives the light weight room to breathe.
  • The italic gets the payoff lines: in heroes, the serif phrase takes its own line(s), colored #263A99, and ends with a period. "Invest in / Operational Intelligence."
  • Section grid: eyebrow sits in a left rail column; headline and content indent to their own column. Section padding clamps 80→165px; 100px desktop gutters.
  • Footer sign-off: the brand line repeats in the footer with the serif italic in orange on navy — ours: "Permanent Capital for Growth."
Where Mercury diverges — on purpose
  • Interactive color: Vista makes #263A99 the button/link color; our current site uses Prussian. Pick one for the new build and hold it — recommendation: adopt Vista's split (navy = text, #263A99 = interactive) so purple stops being "italics only."
  • Body face: Vista sets even body copy in Causten; Mercury uses Inter for running text. Keep Inter — it reads better at small sizes and separates us from a straight clone.
  • Imagery: Vista shows software abstraction — code, data cities, network globes. Mercury shows real rooms and real machines (imaging suites, service benches). Same frame language, opposite subject register — this is our biggest differentiator; protect it.
  • Voice: Vista speaks institutional scale ($103BN AUM). Mercury speaks founder succession and permanence. Borrow the structure, never the vocabulary.

Logo

The mark

A navy square containing the interlocked MP monogram, beside a wide-tracked serif wordmark with a rule between MERCURY and the letter-spaced PARTNERS. Two approved colorways.

Primary — navy on white / light grounds. Used in the site navbar at ~2rem height.

Reversed — white on Prussian Blue. Use on color-scheme-3 surfaces and photography with a navy overlay.

Do

  • ✓Keep clearspace ≥ the height of the monogram square on all sides
  • ✓Minimum width ~160px for the full lockup; below that, use the monogram square alone
  • ✓Place on white, Link Water, Neutral Lightest, or Prussian Blue only

Don't

  • ✕Recolor into orange, purple, or grey
  • ✕Set on mid-tone photography without an overlay
  • ✕Ship placeholder marks — the current site's footer still uses a Relume placeholder logo (see Build Notes)

Our Approach

Refinement for the new build (from Vista): at display sizes, set Causten at weight 200 with negative tracking (≈ −0.02em) and line-height 1, and size the Freight italic em at 1.07em with +1.7px tracking so both faces match optically. The current site's positive heading tracking is the one place it visibly drifts from its reference — see Reference DNA.

Signature Pattern

The section header stack

Every section on the site opens the same way. This four-part stack is the single most recognizable Mercury pattern — carry it into the new build unchanged.

How We Invest 1

Investing to grow while maintaining your legacy 2

Mercury Partners is a long-term investor and operator who will protect the founder's legacy and build on the company's success. 3

See Our Strategy Talk to Us 4
1Eyebrow — orange, uppercase, Inter 700, 0.9rem. Names the topic. 1rem below it.
2Headline — Causten Light with exactly one <em> phrase in Freight italic purple. Italicize the emotional payoff, not a random word.
3Lede — Inter 1.25rem, max ~60ch. States the proof behind the headline.
4Actions — one outlined button + optional text link with arrow. Sentence-case labels.

The compiled site sets em{line-height:.6} to stop Freight's tall metrics from opening up headline leading — replicate that (or set the em's line-height to 1) in the new build.

Imagery

Real rooms, real machines

Photography is documentary, not stock-glossy: partner meetings in daylight offices, and the actual world of the portfolio — MRI suites, imaging components, service benches. Full-color, natural light, square or 4:3 crops, zero radius.

On navy statement sections, photos take a Prussian overlay so the white logo and Malibu links hold contrast. Avoid: handshake stock, skyline clichés, illustration styles — nothing on the current site uses them, and the credibility of the brand depends on the photography looking specific.

Voice & Copy

The founder's language, not the fund's

Copy speaks to founders considering succession, and to the advisors who bring them. It defines Mercury by contrast with traditional PE: permanent vs. fund timelines, operating vs. engineering, legacy vs. exit.

Say

founder-owned · permanent capital · legacy · partnership · operate · long-term owner · lower middle-market · confidential conversation

Avoid

exit-speak (flip, harvest, deploy) · fund jargon (LP, IRR, dry powder) · aggressive CTAs ("Get started now") · exclamation points anywhere

Numbers as proof

Always concrete: $10–$50M revenue, $1–$4M EBITDA, $5–$25M transactions, 25+ years. Numbers stay in Inter, never italicized.

Statement + payoff

Rule of thumb: the italic phrase is what the founder actually cares about. If a headline reads fine without the italic, it's italicizing the wrong words. One <em> per headline, max.

Build Notes

Fix in the new build

Findings from the review of the current Webflow site. None change the look and feel above — but don't carry them forward.

Fix

Placeholder phone number

The Contact page lists +1 (212) 000-0000. Replace with the real number or drop the phone row.

Fix

Relume placeholder logo in the footer

The footer brand image is still relume-710438.png, not the Mercury mark. Use the white lockup on the navy footer.

Fix

Dead footer links

All four footer nav links and the Privacy/Terms links have no targets (linkType: none). Privacy policy and Terms pages don't exist yet.

Fix

Copy typos

"Mecury works to accelerate…" (Home, Grow card) · "THE RIGHT CHOIcE" tagline (Why Mercury) · "…without disrupting operations.." double period (Why Mercury) · duplicated bullet lists between Legacy and Value sections.

Fix

Hijacked design tokens

Two variables were overridden to un-related values: neutral-darkest-15 (a border alpha) → Jacksons Purple, and prussian-blue-light → International Orange. It works visually but will confuse anyone who reuses the tokens. Rebuild the palette with honest names.

Tidy

Heading scale inversion

The H1 element default is 3.5rem while heading-style-h2 is 3.75rem — the class system and element defaults disagree (Causten weights also vary 200–700 across rules). Rationalize into one scale like the table above.

Tidy

Unfinished pages & drafts

"Our Approach" is hidden in the nav and still draft, along with "About Us Copy" and the untouched Relume style-guide page. Decide: ship or delete before launch.

Tidy

SEO & social basics

The Home page has no meta title/description set, and no Open Graph images are configured on any page. Mixed testimonial quote styles (curly vs straight) worth normalizing too.
